# Presentation Design System & Styling Guide (`professional-researcher`)

This guide outlines the visual standards, layout catalog, and multilingual RTL requirements for automated presentation decks generated by the `professional-researcher` engine.

---

## 1. Canvas Dimensions & Aspect Ratio

- **Format**: Modern **16:9 Widescreen** standard (PowerPoint native).
- **Physical Dimensions**: `13.333 inches` width × `7.5 inches` height (`33.86 cm` × `19.05 cm`).
- **Margins**: Safe content zone with `0.8"` left/right padding and `0.45"` top/bottom header-footer padding.

---

## 2. Structural Design Archetypes (v3.0)

Presentations must adapt structurally to the researched domain. Selectable via frontmatter `archetype: ...` or via `--archetype`:

| Archetype | Canvas & Mood | Framing & Geometry | Target Domains |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **`consulting_grid`** (Default) | Soft Slate (`#F8FAFC`) | Structured rounded white cards with subtle borders & accent stripes | Management Consulting, Banking, Enterprise Governance, M&A |
| **`modern_dark`** | Deep Obsidian (`#0A0F1D`) | Dark translucent cards (`#151E2E`), subtle glowing borders (`#2A3854`), electric cyan/violet | AI Labs, Cloud Infrastructure, CyberSecurity, Web3, DevTools |
| **`minimal_editorial`** | Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) | **Frameless / borderless layout**, asymmetric column rules, 52pt+ bold numbers | Luxury, Architecture, High-Level Strategy, Legal, Design |
| **`warm_organic`** | Soft Sand / Cream (`#FBF9F4`)| Organic warm cards, earthy forest/sage green tones, rounded badges | NGOs, Veterinary & Wildlife, Healthcare, Sustainability |
| **`vibrant_bold`** | High-Contrast Accent | Solid accent-filled metric tiles, contrasting breaker cards, high energy | Venture Pitch Decks, Marketing, Product Launches, Keynotes |

## 2.1 Dynamic Domain Design Research Protocol

When in **Dynamic Domain-Adaptive Mode**:
1. The agent searches for real-world slide deck examples in the research domain:
   `search_web(query="presentation slide design examples [domain] color palette pitch deck layouts")`
2. Identifies layout density (e.g., dense technical matrices vs. spacious visual cards).
3. Selects the matching structural archetype (`modern_dark`, `minimal_editorial`, etc.) and derives brand-coherent colors.
4. Generates a presentation adhering 100% to industry-specific design expectations.

## 4. Markdown Slide Syntax & Directives

### Slide Segmentation
Slides are demarcated by horizontal rules (`---` or `***`) or by `## ` headings:

```markdown
---
## Slide Title
<!-- category: Optional Category Badge -->
<!-- layout: content | agenda | two-column | metrics | callout | table | code | image | closing -->

Slide content...
---
```

### Layout Catalog:
1. `content`: Standard slide with paragraphs or multi-level indented bullets (`•`, `–`, `›`).
2. `agenda`: Grid of numbered agenda cards (up to 6 items).
3. `split-hero`: Asymmetric 35/65 layout: prominent hero quote panel on one side and clean numbered items on the other.
4. `versus`: Problem vs. solution battle layout with central floating `VS` badge and status indicator badges.
5. `architecture` / `stack`: 3-tier system topology stack with vertical flow indicators.
6. `timeline` / `process` / `roadmap`: Real horizontal milestone stepper with connecting guidelines and numbered circular node pills.
7. `grid` / `matrix`: 2x2 feature matrix with colored side indicator bars.
8. `two-column`: Side-by-side comparison cards (mirrored automatically in RTL). Use `### Column 1` and `### Column 2`.
9. `metrics`: 2 to 4 KPI cards. Syntax: `- **+45%** Metric Title | Optional subcaption`.
10. `callout`: Prominent alert card with colored left accent bar (`[!NOTE]`, `[!WARNING]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`, `[!TIP]`).
11. `table`: Styled table with colored header, zebra row shading, and cell alignment.
12. `code`: Dark container (`#0F172A`) with monospaced `Consolas` code syntax.
13. `image`: Scaled image with aspect ratio preservation and centered caption.
14. `closing`: Dark concluding slide with strategic takeaways and contact block.

## 4.1 Agentic Custom Slide Generation (Python-PPTX SDK)

When a slide requires a bespoke diagram, flowchart, matrix, or infographic not covered by standard templates:
- The AI Agent does not rely solely on markdown directives.
- The AI Agent writes and executes a specialized Python snippet using `python-pptx` directly to construct the exact custom shapes, connector lines, grouping, and styling required.
- The underlying `pptx_builder.py` exposes helper methods (`_add_slide_background`, `_add_header_footer`, `_render_inline_text`, `_apply_paragraph_rtl`) that custom agentic scripts can reuse directly.

## 5. Multilingual & Right-to-Left (RTL) Standards

* **Auto-Detection**: The engine scans each slide's text for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, or Urdu Unicode characters (`[\u0600-\u06FF]`, etc.).
* **Native OpenXML RTL**: When RTL is detected, `<a:pPr rtl="1"/>` is injected into paragraph XML properties.
* **Complex Script Font**: OpenXML `<a:cs typeface="Arial"/>` is attached to character runs to ensure Arabic glyphs render smoothly.
* **Layout Geometry Mirroring**:
  * Title accent bars appear on the right side rather than the left.
  * In two-column layouts, Column 1 is positioned on the right and Column 2 on the left.
  * Metric cards sequence right-to-left.
  * Running headers and footers mirror their alignment.
